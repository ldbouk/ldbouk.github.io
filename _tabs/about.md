Mar7ba! Mar7ba! I'm a proud Lebanese American breaking into blue team security... the legal way... 

I started this blog for three reasons:

**To cut through the noise.** Cybersecurity content is everywhere, and
most of it assumes you already know what you're doing. Writing things
down forces me to actually understand them, and sorting the signal from
the noise for myself means I can share the signal with you.

**To report what I learn.** This is my public lab notebook: SIEM
experiments, detection attempts, home lab builds, and the mistakes
that come with them.

**To help beginners find their footing.** If you're new to security,
half the battle is figuring out where to even start. You're not alone.
The imposter syndrome is real (mine is typing this right now). I keep
things as simple as possible, partly as a courtesy to you and partly
because my philosophy professors are possibly reading this and I would
rather not receive another very long email about the virtue of
succinctness.

No credentials required.
