Every programmer's first program says "Hello World." Mine has a bit of a Lebanese-American twist. 
If the 7 looks strange, that's because it's **Arabizi**: Arabic written in Latin script, where numbers are stand-ins for sounds
that English letters can't make. The 7 is the letter **ح** (the same sound you make when you say the "h" in "haha"). 

Also, substituting characters to sneak sounds past an alphabet's limits felt very on-brand for a security blog. 
This is also your preview of the humor here. There is no refund policy, but feel free to make me feel validated with a chuckle.

So, mar7ba! I'm Leena, and I'm coming into cybersecurity mid-career.
My journey so far: wanting to be a doctor, then a lawyer (thanks,
high-expectation immigrant parents), then nonprofit fundraising,
event planning, barista work, journalism, managing an independent
bookstore, and owning my own business flipping antique and vintage
furniture (all while trying to communicate the importance of the
historical pieces)... And now, here.


Why cybersecurity? A few reasons. I have a strong sense of justice,
and there is something deeply, almost existentially, satisfying about standing between
ordinary people and the ones trying to take advantage of them. I
also just love puzzles. Digging through logs trying to figure out
what actually happened scratches the same itch as a good mystery
novel.  

Why the blog? For posterity's sake. To help me organize my thoughts. And there's a lot of noise out 
there I'm trying to cut through. Also, as you may have noticed, communicating things I think 
the public should care about is kind of my whole career pattern. So this blog is where I write it down. 
Expect home lab experiments, SIEM adventures, detection attempts, and honest accounts of what went wrong.

(First honest account: this site 404'd at launch, and I have no idea
how long that lasted because I didn't check on it all weekend. Day
one and I've already invented a monitoring gap. Clearly a natural!)

If you're also new to this field: mar7ba! You're in the right place.
No credentials required.
